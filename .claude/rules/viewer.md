# fmShowcase, the showcase viewer

Loaded when a session works on the viewer, its read-only canvas or showcase files. The brief, approved by the owner on 27 October 2026 with all 11 of its recommendations, is `docs/viewer-brief.md`; its phases are V1 (done on branch `claude/viewer-v1`) to V6. Keep this file in step with the code, as `CLAUDE.md` asks.

- **What it is** (phases V1 and V2): `src/viewer/` → `apps/fmViewer.html`, the app called **fmShowcase** on screen (the file people open is "a viewer"). Apache 2.0 like fmGraph, with its licence notice at the top of `index.html`; its help text CC BY 4.0 (`src/help/viewer-help.js`). **Made only from fmGraph's board engine, `src/shared/` and its own: no ExcelExporter code, ever** (group 91 checks the build's list of its sources). **Read-only**: no editing control exists in the page (not hidden: absent), and nothing a visitor does changes the model (`fmViewer.modelText()` stays `modelTextAtLoad()`). It makes no network calls; what a visitor moved is kept on the device only (below).
- **The Board** (phase V2, 28 October 2026; `docs/viewer-brief.md`, "How V2 turned out"): the viewer takes in **fmGraph's board engine** by name in `js/01-core.js` (`// build:include fmgraph/js/…`, the list group 92's `ENGINE`: reading boards with the board file's upgrades, the calculation with sliders, every bar's and chart's figures and SVG, scenarios, Play's motion, A/B, Trace's reach, Biggest movers, the sample; `.claude/rules/fmgraph.md`) and **never one of fmGraph's other fragments**; its `js/06-board.js` defines the engine's **page interface** (`isPhone`, `renderBoard`, `updateValues`, `scheduleUpdate`, `paintMark`, `rectKeySwatch` / `chartKeySwatch` — plain marks, no colour box — `freeGridHook` — nothing — and in `07-scenarios-play.js` `renderCompareBar`, `renderPlayBar`, `paintPlay`), with fmGraph's class names (`.widget`, `.bar-widget`, `.chart-widget`, `.slider-widget`, `#board`, `#barList`, `.bar-chart-host`, `.chart-key`, `.bar-errors`) so the engine's painting works unchanged; the drawing's styles are `src/fmgraph/board.css`, the page's `src/viewer/board-view.css`. A showcase with boards (`boards`, an `fmIDE-graph-board` file — `readBoards`: limits, kind, upgrades, a newer one asked about, the template form refused, `cleanBoards`, empty boards left out), else a document's own `graphBoards` (the owner's choice 10), opens on the **Board** (`#boardView`; Board | Logic `#viewSwitch` `#tabBoard` / `#tabLogic`, on a phone `#phoneViews` at the bottom); without, on the Logic view as in V1.
